SPAM DVC Course

Learn how SPAM tracks bone inside a CT scan, one small idea at a time.

Hi, I'm Vox, a very small cube of bone scan. My job is to find out where each piece of bone went when the vertebra was squeezed. Press the buttons, drag the sliders and break things. That's how this works best.

How each lesson works

  • Vox explains the idea in everyday words.
  • Labs let you try it. The numbers are calculated live, using the same maths as SPAM but on small 2D images.
  • Real SPAM boxes show where the idea lives in SPAM's code and in its output files.
The lessons were written from the code of SPAM 0.8.1.4, mainly spam/DIC/registration.py, ldic.py, pixelSearch.py and grid.py. The heaviest calculations are compiled, so a few low-level details could not be read directly. The labs use made-up 2D images and solve 2 numbers per window, where SPAM solves 12. The video in Lesson 7 is real SPAM running on a made-up 3D volume.

Lesson 5

Speed Flags

-np sets how many workers run at the same time.

More workers, faster job

Painting a long fence alone takes a whole day. Four friends finish much sooner, because each paints their own bit. SPAM's windows are like fence panels: each is a separate job. But friends get in each other's way, so 100 friends won't finish in a minute!
$ spam-ldic unloaded.tif loaded.tif -np 4 -pf reg-registration.tsv -hws 25 -ns 25

If you leave out -np, SPAM uses every core your computer has, and it can make your laptop hot and loud.

Real timings

Going from 1 to 4 workers was 3.5 times faster, but going from 4 to 6 only saved about a second. The same answers come out whichever number you pick, since -np only changes how fast they arrive.

How to choose

SituationTry
Laptop while you're using itAbout half your cores
Quick test on a small scan2 to 4
Server or clusterThe cores you've been given
Out of memoryFewer workers, or use -lazy
Workers come from Python's multiprocessing.Pool(processes). Nodes are handed out as workers become free, and they finish in any order. Results are still saved against the right node. -lazy reads the images from disk through tifffile.memmap, instead of loading them fully into memory.

Remember this

  • -np changes speed, not the answer.
  • Extra workers help less and less.
  • On a shared machine, always set it yourself.

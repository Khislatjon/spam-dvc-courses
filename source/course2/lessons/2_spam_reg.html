Lesson 2

Registration Flags

-bb says how blurry to begin, and -be says how sharp to end.

The basic command

$ spam-reg unloaded.tif loaded.tif -mf1 mask.tif -bb 4 -be 2 -od . -pre reg
Remember the blurry glasses? -bb means binning begin, and -be means binning end. -bb 4 -be 2 says: "Start at bin 4, then finish at bin 2." Use powers of 2: 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16.

Starting sharp fails here: -bb 1 misses by over 16 voxels after 50 iterations. Once you start at bin 4, going higher adds nothing. Finishing at bin 1 took more than twice as long and wasn't better, so stopping at bin 2 is a good choice here. Don't worry about the miss of about 4.6 voxels. Registration only gives one Phi for the whole sample, and the local bulge can't fit into it. It's just the first guess.

How to choose

SituationTry

Very large movement-bb 8 or more
Noisy image or a small sampleBe careful with high binning, which can erase the pattern
-rig finds only movement and rotation, with no stretch. -it sets the iteration limit, default 50. -dp sets the stopping step, default 0.0001 for spam-reg, while spam-ldic uses 0.001. -g shows iterations in a graph window.

Remember this

  • Check for returnStatus 2 before going on.
  • Registration is a first guess, so it doesn't need to be perfect.
  • Start blurry enough, but don't overdo it.

Lesson 6

The Army of Windows

One Phi for the whole bone is too simple. Local DVC gives every small region its own.

A team of tiny detectives

Vox saysRegistration is like one general describing the whole battle. Local DVC sends out thousands of little detectives, one for each spot in the bone. Each detective takes the general's report as a hint, then works out what really happened at their own spot. They don't talk to each other, so they can all work at the same time.

Change the settings, then press Run. This is a made-up 2D vertebra with a lesion on the right side, and the area near the lesion moved more than the rest.

The two main dials

SettingWhat it doesBigger meansExample
Node spacing, -nsDistance between nodesFewer nodes and less detail, but faster50 vx
Half-window, -hwsVoxels each side of the nodeMore texture and less noise, but less local25 vx

Window width = 2 × hws + 1. So hws 25 gives 51 voxels, about 2 mm. With a spacing of 50 and a width of 51, windows barely overlap, so each node is close to an independent measurement.

Vox saysSmall windows see tiny details, but may have too little pattern to be sure. Big windows are confident, but they average everything inside. It's like asking one person or a whole crowd: the crowd is steadier, but it can't tell you what one person thinks.

What happens at each node

OrderStepCode
1Build the grid. The first node sits one spacing in from the edge.makeGrid

3Cut the window, and skip it if it's too masked or too dark.getImagettes

Real SPAM · spam/DIC/ldic.py
Each node is a separate job, sent to multiprocessing.Pool(processes) and collected with imap_unordered, so nodes finish in any order. That's what -np 4 controls. A whole-voxel part of the guess is used to cut the loaded window in the right place, and SPAM keeps only the small remainder to refine, which saves work. getImagettes also returns status −5 when too much of a window is masked. With -mc 0.5, at least half of a window must be unmasked. The -glt option also skips dark windows, but only if air is darker than the limit you set, so check the grey value of air in your scans first.
Vox saysMasks can save a lot of time. An air window has no real pattern, so it often runs right up to the iteration limit, while bone windows finish quickly. A scan with lots of air can spend most of its time on nothing!

Remember this

  • Each node gets its own window and its own Phi.
  • Registration gives the starting guess.
  • The workers don't help each other, so nodes run in parallel.
  • A mask stops SPAM wasting time on air.

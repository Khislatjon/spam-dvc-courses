How far can you trust it?

Vox saysBeing a good detective means knowing what you don't know. A finished displacement field looks very convincing, but each kind of check only catches certain mistakes.

Real SPAM · spam-filterPhiField
Strong strain changes a window's pattern too, so collapse lowers correlation even for a good answer. SPAM also has an optional filter that can replace poor nodes using their neighbours. A filtered field looks tidier, but those values are filled in, not measured, so keep track of which nodes they are.

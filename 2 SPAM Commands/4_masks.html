Lesson 4

Mask Flags

Tell SPAM where not to look. It saves time and removes rubbish results.

Painting over the air

Vox saysImagine searching for your friend in a stadium, but they told you they're in section B. You'd ignore all the others. A mask is that hint. It's a picture the same size as your scan: 0 means "ignore this", and anything else means "use this".
$ spam-ldic unloaded.tif loaded.tif -mf1 mask.tif -mc 0.5 -pf reg-registration.tsv -hws 25 -ns 50
FlagMeansDefault
-mf1Mask file for image 1, a TIFF the same size as the scannone
-mcFraction of a window that must be unmasked0.5
-gltSkip a window if its average grey is below this valueoff

The name means a mask on image 1 only. It uses your reference scan, so make the mask from that one. spam-reg accepts -mf1 too.

What does -mc do?

Drag the slider. The yellow window sits at the edge of a sample. It's used only if enough of it is unmasked.

Real SPAM · spam/DIC/grid.py
The test is (imagette1mask != 0).mean() >= minMaskCoverage. So -mc 0.5 keeps a window when at least half of it is unmasked. The help text words it differently, so this is worth knowing. If the window fails the test, it gets status −5 and SPAM skips it.

Vox saysWithout a mask, 10 windows hit the iteration limit and 12 were wrong, and they were all doing pointless work on air and edges. As -mc rises, you lose nodes near the surface. Set it too high and you throw away good edge nodes. 0.5 is a sensible start.

No mask? Try -glt

-glt means grey low threshold. It skips dark windows, with no mask file needed. But it only works if you know how dark your air is. Here, air averaged about 21 grey and the sample about 126.

Setting (no mask)Nodes runSkippedWhat happened
-glt 5640Below the air value, so it skipped nothing

Vox saysCheck the grey value of air in your scan before picking a number. A value that worked for someone else's scanner may do nothing on yours.

Remember this

  • -mf1 gives the mask, and 0 means ignore.
  • -mc is the fraction that must be unmasked.
  • -glt is a quick option, but check your air value first.

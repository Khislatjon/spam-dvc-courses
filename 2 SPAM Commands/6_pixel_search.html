Lesson 6

Pixel Search Flags

When local movement is large, set how far to search with -sr, and keep your scaling right with -pfb.

Do you need pixel search?

Vox saysDon't start with pixel search. It's slower, and most runs don't need it. Run normally first, then look for warning signs. Only add a search if they point to a problem.
StepWhat to do
1Run spam-reg and then spam-ldic, as in Lesson 3.
2Check the warning signs below.
3If several signs gather in one region, add a pixel search and run spam-ldic again.

A two-step run

Searching at full size is very slow, so a common plan is to search half-size images, then refine at full size. That needs half-size copies of your scans and mask, made by averaging each 2 × 2 × 2 block, and three commands:

# 1. One Phi for the whole scan, at full size $ spam-reg unloaded.tif loaded.tif -mf1 mask.tif -bb 4 -be 2 -pre reg # 2. Search on half-size images, so ns and hws are halved too $ spam-pixelSearch u2.tif l2.tif -mf1 m2.tif -pf reg-registration.tsv -pfb 0.5 \ -sr -5 5 -18 18 -5 5 -ns 25 -hws 12 -pre ps # 3. Refine at full size, starting from the search $ spam-ldic unloaded.tif loaded.tif -mf1 mask.tif -pf ps-pixelSearch.tsv -pfb 2 \ -ns 50 -hws 25 -pre final

-sr: how far to search

It takes six numbers: minimum and maximum in z, then y, then x, in voxels of the image being searched. They are measured from the current guess.

Vox says-sr -5 5 -18 18 -5 5 means "look 5 voxels either way in z, 18 in y and 5 in x." On half-size images, that's really 10, 36 and 10 voxels. Searching a long thin box is far quicker than a big cube.

-pfb: keeping the scale right

A displacement of 10 voxels at half size is 20 voxels at full size. -pfb tells SPAM how to scale a Phi file.

-pfb = (size of the current images) ÷ (size of the images that made the file)
File came fromUsed on-pfb
Full sizeHalf size0.5
Half sizeFull size2
Same sizeSame size1 (default)

Two real traps

Trap 1: forget -pfb but keep -ns

spam-ldic: you passed a -ns=[50, 50, 50] which contradicts the node spacing in your Phi Field TSV of [25. 25. 25.] hint 1: if you pass a Phi Field TSV you don't need to also define the node spacing

This one is helpful, but SPAM still exits as if all went well and writes no results file. If your script carries on, it will fail later, so read the output.

Trap 2: forget both -pfb and -ns

This is worse. It runs with no complaint, and the numbers look fine. But the nodes keep their half-size positions, at 25, 50, 75 and 100 voxels, so it measures only one corner of the sample. In the real test, just 19 nodes converged, and the rest of the sample was never measured.

Vox saysBoth traps are about mixing sizes. After any run, check two things: how many nodes you got, and whether they cover your whole sample.

Remember this

  • -sr takes six numbers: z min, z max, y min, y max, x min, x max.
  • The range is in voxels of the images being searched.
  • -pfb is new size ÷ old size.
  • Halve -ns and -hws on half-size images.
  • Search only when warning signs gather in one region, then check again.
